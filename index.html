Quick Start

Get online in under 30 seconds

1

Install & Launch

Download `StatusFlow-Setup.exe` and complete the quick 2-click setup. A clean shortcut is placed right on your desktop with zero clutter.

2

Pick Photo / GIF & Text

Click "Choose images / GIF" or paste any image directly with Ctrl+V from clipboard (including Win+Shift+S screenshots), then type your Name and Description.

3

Click Start

Hit the green Start button. Your Discord profile immediately displays your new custom status!
